Chicken Orzo Bake Recipe


Home


Description

Chicken orzo bake is a cozy, one-pan casserole featuring tender chicken, rice-shaped orzo pasta, and savory broth baked together until creamy. It often includes garlic, parmesan, spinach, and sun-dried tomatoes.


Ingredients

  • 4 to 6 boneless chicken thighs
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil, divided
  • 2 tablespoons Dijon mustard, divided
  • 1 generous teaspoon dried Italian herbs
  • garlic to taste (chopped or minced)
  • salt and pepper to taste
  • butter
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 1 cup orzo
  • 1 cup chicken stock
  • 3 tablespoons cream
  • 1 teaspoon each chopped fresh thyme and tarragon (if available)
